Policy that would apply in production — not in force in this demo

  • A soft credit inquiry only. Soft inquiries do not affect a credit score and are not visible to other lenders.
  • Information is used solely to assess SBA 7(a) eligibility. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest for the duration of the review.
  • Records are securely deleted by each participating lender within 90 days of the review closing, and a deletion confirmation is sent to the applicant.
  • Authorization may be withdrawn at any time before submission.

Lender matches

How to read these

  • Approves in-house means the SBA has given that bank authority to decide on its own. Their yes is final — there is no second gate.
  • Needs SBA sign-off means a second review after the bank approves — typically two to four more weeks, and a gate that can reopen questions.
  • Time to term sheet is how long until you have written terms in hand. Time to funding is the whole run from signed letter of intent to money in escrow. Both are in days.
  • Watch this against your exclusivity window. Most LOIs give 60 to 90 days. A lender whose median close exceeds your window means asking the seller for an extension — after your diligence money is already spent.
  • The line at the bottom of each card is not a timing figure — it is how many loans those medians were calculated from. A bank doing three deals a quarter has a thin record; one doing ten a month does this every day.

How these numbers were produced

Rates are illustrative, from published SBA 7(a) spread caps at a 6.75% prime rate — loans over $350,000 are capped at prime + 3.0%. Fees represent guaranty, packaging, valuation, and closing costs.

Timing would be the median of each lender’s own closings over the trailing twelve months, with the sample size shown so a thin record is visible rather than hidden. The dominant driver is Preferred Lender Program status: PLP lenders hold delegated authority and approve without SBA pre-review, while standard lenders submit to SBA and add roughly two to four weeks. Published ranges put PLP 7(a) at three to six weeks against four to ten for standard, and clean acquisitions at sixty to ninety days from agreement.

Note the trade-off this makes visible: the lowest rate here is also the slowest close. Against a seventy-five day exclusivity window, that gap can matter more than the spread.
